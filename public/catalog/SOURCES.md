The following `ai-*.jpg` assets are controlled garment-focused crops derived from the
listed source photographs. They are sent to GPT Image 2 as construction references;
they intentionally remove most faces and full scenes so the model does not replace
the customer's identity or background. The original photographs remain available for
the visual showroom cards.
